How It Works

You tell us what you're after. We map the right path.

Renovating a laundry is a bigger decision than it looks — plumbing, waterproofing, tiling and cabinetry all have to land in the right order, or you pay for it later.

Here's our approach: we start with one honest conversation about what your laundry actually needs. If it's cabinetry — storage, benchtops, better workflow — our own team designs, builds and installs it end to end.

If it needs more — plumbing moved, new tiling, a full strip-out — we bring in renovation specialists we know and trust. We've built these relationships over years of working alongside Sydney's leading renovation companies, and we only refer work to people we'd let into our own homes.

If you're after a quick surface-level freshen-up, our cabinet team handles that directly. If you want the full transformation, this is where our partner network earns its keep.

Good Questions

Laundry renovation FAQs

Who actually does the renovation work?

Snap designs the space and handles all cabinetry in-house. Licensed work — plumbing, waterproofing, tiling, electrical — is delivered by renovation partners we've worked alongside for years. Each partner is licensed and insured for their trade, and we only refer work to people we'd use in our own homes.

Do I have to manage multiple trades?

No — that's the point. One conversation with us scopes the project, and you keep one point of contact while the right specialists do their part in the right order.

How much does a laundry renovation cost?

Cabinetry-only refreshes typically land between $2,500–$6,500. Full renovations vary with plumbing, tiling and layout changes — most sit in the $8,000–$25,000 range. We'll give you a clear scope and numbers after the consultation, before anything is committed.

What if I only need cabinets?

Then you only pay for cabinets — see our Laundry Cabinets page. We'll tell you honestly if a full renovation isn't needed.
