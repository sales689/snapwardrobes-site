Match the floor, not the wall

The most natural-looking wardrobes pick up the tone of your flooring. Warm oak floors love a Nordic Oak or Soft Walnut; grey or dark floors sit beautifully with charcoal or crisp white.

Small or dark room? Keep it light

In compact or low-light bedrooms, white melamine or a pale oak keeps everything feeling open and airy — and it's the safe, resale-friendly choice.

Statement or timeless

A painted 2-Pac colour or deep woodgrain turns the robe into a feature. Renovating to sell? A clean neutral is the smarter long-term call. Either way, we'll help you choose at the measure.

Good Questions

Built-in wardrobe FAQs

Are these package prices what I'll actually pay?

They're genuine indicative from-prices for a typical 2.4m opening at each finish level. Your room's exact width, height and any site quirks set the final number — which we fix in writing at your design consultation, before you commit to anything.

Sliding or hinged — which is better?

Sliding doors preserve floor space and span wider openings, which usually means fewer doors and a lower cost. Hinged Shaker doors give the premium Hamptons look and full visibility into the wardrobe, but need clearance to open. If your bed sits close to the wardrobe wall, sliding is usually the smarter call.

Do you remove my old wardrobe?

Yes — just ask and we handle takeout and disposal. Removal can expose unpainted wall, which is why the Classic package and up include backing panels for a fully finished interior.

What goes in first — the wardrobe or new flooring?

Carpet: wardrobe first, carpet fitted around it. Timber or floating floors: flooring first. We'll confirm the right order for your renovation at the measure.

How much does a built-in wardrobe cost in Sydney?

Most built-in wardrobes land from around $980 inc GST for a standard sliding-door fit-out, up to about $11,980 for a fully painted, LED-lit design — your opening width, door type, finish and internals set where you sit. Our custom wardrobe cost guide breaks down exactly what moves the price.

Do you make built-in wardrobes in my area?

Yes — we're a family-run Sydney business and design, make and install across the Hills District, North West Sydney, Lower North Shore, Inner West and the Eastern Suburbs. See the areas we service, or compare a walk-in wardrobe or sliding doors if you're weighing options.
