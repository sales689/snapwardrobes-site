How much does a custom wardrobe cost in Sydney?

A straight answer with real 2026 price ranges — what pushes the cost up or down, what's included, and where it's actually worth spending.

Pricing Guide · Updated August 2026 · 6 min read

"How much is a wardrobe?" is the first question almost everyone asks — and the honest answer is: it depends on the space, the doors, the finish and how much storage you pack inside. Because a custom wardrobe is made to measure, there's no single sticker price. But you don't want vague, so here are the real ranges we work to in Sydney, and exactly what moves them.

All prices below are inc GST and are "from" figures for a typical job. Your exact price is set in writing after a free in-home measure.

The short answer

Most custom wardrobes in Sydney land in these bands:

  • Built-in wardrobe: from around $980 for a standard fit-out, up to $12,000+ for a high-end painted, LED-lit design.
  • Walk-in wardrobe: from around $1,690 for a simple layout, up to $17,000+ for a full boutique fit-out with an island.
  • Sliding doors only: from around $250 per door for mirror or white, up to $650 per door for painted Shaker-profile.

Where you sit in those bands comes down to a handful of choices.

What actually drives the price

Two wardrobes the same width can be thousands apart. Here's what makes the difference:

  • Size of the opening. Wider and taller means more board, more doors and more labour.
  • Door type. Sliding mirror or white is the value option. Hinged Shaker doors, framed or frameless glass, and painted profiles cost more.
  • Finish. White melamine is the base. Polytec colours and woodgrains step it up; 2-Pac painted (any colour) is the premium.
  • Internals. Extra drawers, soft-close runners, shoe shelving and hanging combinations add up — drawers are the single biggest internal cost.
  • Extras. LED lighting, backing panels, kickboards and removal of an old wardrobe each add to the total.
  • Access. Tricky access, tall ceilings and awkward walls take more time to install.

Sliding doors on their own

If your internals are fine and you just want to replace tired doors, sliding doors are priced per door:

  • Mirror or white melamine — from $250/door
  • Framed frosted or grey mirror — from $320/door
  • Frameless mirror or super white glass — from $420/door
  • Painted Shaker-profile — from $650/door

You can price a set on our sliding doors page.

What about kitchens and laundries?

Different job, different scale. As a rough guide: a laundry cabinetry refresh is priced on size, finishes and materials, while custom kitchens typically run from around $8,000 for a cabinetry refresh up to $45,000+ for a full renovation. We quote both properly after a measure.

Where it's worth spending (and where it isn't)

If the budget's tight, this is where your money works hardest:

  • Spend on drawers and soft-close. It's what you touch every day, and it's the upgrade people notice most.
  • Spend on getting the layout right. Good design beats expensive finishes — the right mix of hanging, shelving and drawers is what makes a wardrobe feel bigger.
  • You can save on finish. White melamine internals behind good doors look clean and cost far less than colour or painted.
  • Don't cheap out on install. A perfectly made wardrobe fitted poorly looks poorly made. Measured properly and installed square is what makes it last.
Your wardrobe usually isn't too small — it was just never designed properly. Layout, not size, is what makes the difference.

Frequently asked

Are built-in wardrobes worth it?

For most Sydney homes, yes. A fitted wardrobe uses the full height and width of the space, so you get far more usable storage than a freestanding unit — and it looks built-in rather than added on. Buyers also see it as a fixed improvement, which helps at resale.

Do custom wardrobes add value to your home?

Good storage is one of the things buyers notice. A well-designed built-in or walk-in makes bedrooms feel larger and more finished, and it presents as a permanent upgrade rather than furniture, which supports the sale price.

What's the cheapest custom option?

A standard built-in with white melamine internals and sliding mirror or white doors is the most affordable custom option, from around $980 inc GST for a typical opening. Flat-pack is cheaper again, but it isn't made to measure and rarely uses the space as well.

Is the price fixed?

Yes. After the free measure we give you a fixed, itemised quote inc GST. The design and measurements are confirmed on site, so the price you accept is the price you pay.
