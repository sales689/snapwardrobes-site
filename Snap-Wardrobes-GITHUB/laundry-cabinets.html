Designed With Purpose

The hardest-working room deserves the best-planned storage.

The laundry is where the whole household passes through — yet in most Sydney homes it's an afterthought. A lone tub, a machine, and washing powder living on the floor.

We design laundry cabinetry the way we design wardrobes: around how the space is actually used. Where the basket lands. Where detergent lives. Where the ironing board hides. Where the mop goes so you never see it.

Layouts, finishes and materials are chosen for how the room works day to day — not short-lived trends.

If you're after a single floating shelf, any handyman can help. If you want the laundry properly planned, built and finished — you're in the right place.

Good Questions

Laundry cabinet FAQs

How much do custom laundry cabinets cost?

Every laundry is priced on what you actually want — it depends on the size of the space, the finishes you choose and the quality of the materials and internals. Your in-home design consultation gives you a clear, fixed price based on exactly that.

Do the cabinets handle moisture?

Yes — we use moisture-resistant boards suited to laundry conditions, with finishes chosen for wear and wipe-down cleaning.

Can you match my kitchen or wardrobe finishes?

Absolutely. We work with the full Polytec range, so your laundry can match cabinetry anywhere else in the home.

What if my laundry needs plumbing or tiling work?

That's a laundry renovation — which we also handle, delivered with our licensed renovation partners. One conversation, and we'll scope which path your project needs.
