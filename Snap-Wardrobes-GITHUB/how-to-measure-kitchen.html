How to measure your kitchen

A kitchen has more moving parts than a wardrobe, but the method is the same: sketch the room, measure the walls, mark the services. Here's how to do it simply.

Start the Guide Print This Guide
✓ Measure in millimetres (mm)
✓ A rough sketch is all we need to start
✓ We site-check every measurement before manufacture
Before You Start

What you'll need

01

A tape measure

A 5m or 8m metal tape. For long walls, a second person helps keep it straight.

02

Pen & paper

Or print this page. Sketch the whole room, even the bits without cabinets.

03

Fifteen minutes

Kitchens take a little longer because of the services and appliances.

04

Your appliance list

Note any appliances you're keeping or buying, so we design around their sizes.

The Five Steps

Measure it like this

  1. Sketch the room as a box

    Draw every wall — even ones without cabinets — plus any protrusions, chimney breasts or bulkheads. This becomes your plan.

  2. Mark the openings and services

    Show doors, windows and archways. Then mark the E electrical, G gas, water and waste points, plus any meters, fuse box or air-con — anything that affects where cabinets and appliances can go.

  3. Label and measure each wall

    Letter your walls A B C and measure each length. Then measure the floor-to-ceiling height (and to any bulkhead).

  4. Measure the vertical heights

    Floor to window, floor to waste, and floor to the water source. These decide bench and cabinet heights around your services.

  5. Mark the sink and appliances

    Show where the sink, oven, cooktop, rangehood and fridge sit — or where you'd like them. Even a rough idea helps us plan the layout.

Write It Down

Your kitchen measurements

Fill this in and send it through with a photo of your sketch. This gets us started — we confirm everything precisely at your in-home design consultation.

  • A · Wall lengthmm
  • Floor to ceiling / bulkheadmm
  • B · Wall lengthmm
  • Floor to windowmm
  • C · Wall lengthmm
  • Window to wall lengthmm
  • Doorway to wall lengthmm
  • Floor to waste heightmm
  • Floor to water sourcemm
  • Services stack measurementmm
A Few Pointers

Get it right the first time

Measure to the architraves

For doors and windows, measure to the outside of the frame, then across it — and note the frame width.

Don't forget the ceiling

Overhead cabinets and rangehoods need the height. Note any bulkheads or sloped ceilings.

Photograph everything

Snap a few photos of each wall and the services. Pictures fill the gaps a sketch can miss.
