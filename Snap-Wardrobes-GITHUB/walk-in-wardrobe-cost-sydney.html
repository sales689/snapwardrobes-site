How much does a walk-in wardrobe cost in Sydney? (2026 guide)

Real price bands, what actually moves the number, and how much room you need. Written by the team that measures, designs and installs them.

Pricing Guide · Updated October 2026 · 7 min read

Search "walk-in wardrobe cost" and you'll find numbers anywhere from a couple of thousand dollars to $30,000. Both ends are real. The range is wide because "walk-in" covers everything from a simple open layout in a spare room to a fully fitted dressing room with doors, lighting and an island. This guide breaks down what Sydney homeowners actually pay, what drives the price, and where the money is worth spending.

The short answer

For a custom, made-to-measure walk-in in Sydney, most families spend between about $4,000 and $9,000. A simple, well-planned layout can come in under $2,000. A full dressing room in 2-Pac with doors and lighting starts around $17,000 and goes up from there.

What actually changes the price

Walk-ins are priced on how much cabinetry you need and what it is made from. In order of impact:

  • Finish. White melamine is the most cost-effective. A Polytec colour or woodgrain adds to it. 2-Pac painted in any colour is the premium finish and the biggest single jump. Our finishes and colours guide explains the difference.
  • Doors or open shelving. Open walk-ins cost less. Adding cabinet doors, even to a few sections, adds hinges, panels and labour.
  • Drawers. Each soft-close drawer is one of the more expensive components. Six drawers costs noticeably more than two.
  • Size and layout. A galley (both sides) or U-shape uses more lineal metres than a single run or an L-shape.
  • Extras. LED strip lighting, an island bench, shoe tilt-outs, jewellery inserts and mirrors all add up.
  • The room itself. Ceiling height, sloping ceilings, bulkheads, windows and removing an old robe all affect the job.

Where to spend, where to save

Spend on the layout and the drawers you'll use every day. Save on finishes in areas nobody sees and on extras you can add later. A clever layout in white melamine will out-perform an expensive finish on a poor layout every time.

Walk-in vs built-in: why walk-ins cost more

Per metre, a walk-in and a built-in cost about the same. The difference is quantity. A built-in wardrobe runs along one wall. A walk-in wraps around two or three, so you are often fitting out twice the cabinetry. If you're still deciding between the two, read our built-in vs walk-in comparison.

How much space you need

Hanging clothes need about 600mm of depth. Add a clear walkway of at least 900mm and you get the rough minimums:

  • One side fitted out: about 1.5m wide.
  • Both sides (galley): about 2.1m wide or more.
  • U-shape or island: a small spare bedroom or larger.

If the space is tighter than that, a well-designed built-in with a walk-in feel is often the better answer. Squeezing a walk-in into too little room is the most common mistake we see.

Typical Sydney scenarios

  • Master ensuite walk-through in a new Hills District home: usually Essential or Classic. The builder's space is fine; it just needs a proper layout.
  • Spare bedroom converted to a dressing room: usually Classic or Premium. More wall to fit out, and clients often want drawers and some doors.
  • Renovated master suite in an established home: often Premium or Signature, with colour, doors and lighting to match the rest of the renovation.

What isn't included in a cheap quote

When comparing quotes, check the basics are actually in the number: measure and design, delivery, installation, end panels, kickboards, drawer hardware quality, and removal of the old robe. A low headline price that leaves these out often ends up costing more. Every Snap walk-in includes the design consultation, soft-close hardware and a 10-year workmanship warranty.

Your walk-in usually isn't too small. It was just never designed properly.

Frequently asked

How much does a walk-in wardrobe cost in Sydney?

As a guide, a custom walk-in from Snap Wardrobes starts from $1,690 inc GST for a simple open layout in white melamine, sits around $3,990 to $8,990 for most family homes, and runs from $16,990 for a fully fitted dressing room with doors, 2-Pac and lighting. Your room's size and the inclusions you choose set the final price, fixed in writing at the design consultation.

Is a walk-in wardrobe more expensive than a built-in?

Usually, yes. A walk-in wraps cabinetry around two or three walls instead of one, so there is simply more of it. Per metre the cost is similar; the difference is how many metres you are fitting out.

What is the minimum size for a walk-in wardrobe?

Allow about 600mm for hanging cabinetry plus a clear walkway of at least 900mm. That means roughly 1.5m wide for storage down one side and about 2.1m or more to fit out both sides comfortably.

What adds the most to the cost of a walk-in?

Finish and doors. Moving from white melamine to a Polytec colour or 2-Pac paint, adding cabinet doors, extra soft-close drawers, LED lighting or an island are the biggest price movers. The layout itself is rarely the expensive part.

Can I start with a simple walk-in and add to it later?

Yes. Many clients start with a well-planned layout and add drawers, shoe storage or lighting later. Designing it properly from day one is what makes those upgrades easy, because the carcasses and spacing are already right.
