Colours & Finishes

The colour is where it becomes yours.

Your package decides how far your colour choice can go. Essential and Classic are built in clean white melamine. Premium opens up the full Polytec colour range across carcass and cabinet doors. Signature unlocks any colour at all, hand-sprayed in 2-Pac. Whichever way you go, we bring physical samples to your measure so you can see them in your own light before you decide.

Essential & Classic

Crisp white melamine

Moisture-resistant white melamine HMR — clean, bright and a genuine designer favourite. It makes a walk-in feel larger and lighter, never dates, and stays scratch-resistant and colour-stable for the life of the room.

Match the floor, not the wall

The most natural-looking robes pick up the tone of your flooring. Warm oak floors love a Nordic Oak or Soft Walnut; grey or dark floors sit beautifully with charcoal or crisp white.

Light it right

A walk-in has no window borrowing daylight, so paler finishes and LED lighting keep it bright and easy to use. Darker woodgrains look stunning but want good lighting to shine.

Statement or timeless

A painted 2-Pac colour or deep woodgrain makes the dressing room a feature. Renovating to sell? A clean neutral is the smarter long-term call. Either way, we'll help you choose at the measure.

Not Sure Which?

Here's the honest guide.

Essential if the layout is the problem — you want everything to finally have a place, at the sharpest price.

Classic if you'll use it every day for years — soft-close and shoe storage are the two upgrades people never regret.

Premium if the robe should look finished from every angle — colour, backing and doors make it furniture, not fit-out.

Signature if this is the master suite moment — lighting, painted finishes and an island make it the best room in the house.

Good Questions

Walk-in wardrobe FAQs

Are these the final prices?

They're honest indicative starting points for typical rooms. Your room's size and shape set the final number, which we fix in writing at your design consultation — before you commit to anything.

Can I mix packages?

Absolutely — the packages are starting points, not boxes. Want Classic internals with LED lighting? Essential layout with a Polytec island? Your designer prices exactly what you want.

Can you convert a spare room into a walk-in?

Yes — spare rooms and generous alcoves convert beautifully. We design around the room's shape, windows and doorways.

What's the difference between woodmatt and 2-Pac painted?

Polytec woodmatt is a textured timber-look board — durable and mid-range. 2-Pac polyurethane is spray-painted in any Dulux colour with a furniture-grade finish — the premium option, and the true Hamptons look.
