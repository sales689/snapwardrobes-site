Step 01

Choose your finish

Every glass finish comes framed or frameless. Our board and painted finishes come framed. This sets your per-door price.

Step 02

Framed or frameless

Framed sits the glass in a slim aluminium frame — robust and sharper on price. Frameless is edge-to-edge with polished edges — cleaner lines, more premium.

Step 03

Frame & track colour

Powder-coated aluminium frames, tracks and trims. Matte silver and white are standard; matte black and polished chrome are a small upgrade per door.

Frameless doors have no frame — so this colours your track and trims only (the glass sits edge-to-edge). Same colour range, no per-door frame charge.

Step 04

Your openings

Add a row for each wardrobe you're changing and choose how many doors span each opening. If you have them, pop in the height and width in millimetres (floor to top, wall to wall) — this unlocks free delivery.

Openings over 2400mm high are oversize — a per-door surcharge applies (maximum height 2700mm).

Framed or Frameless?

The 10-second explainer.

  • Framed — glass or mirror sits in a slim aluminium frame. Robust, classic, sharper price.
  • Frameless — edge-to-edge glass with polished edges. Cleaner lines, more premium, costs more.
  • Black frame upgrade — turns any framed door architectural. Small cost, big look.
  • Doors only or full package — happy internals? We'll replace just the doors and tracks.

Good Questions

Sliding door FAQs

What's included in the per-door price?

The door itself in your chosen finish. Frames and tracks are from $50 per opening, and the black frame upgrade is a small add-on per door. Your in-home design consultation locks the full fixed price for the opening.

Can you replace just the doors on my existing wardrobe?

Yes — if your internals are fine, we replace only the doors and tracks, custom cut to your opening.

How wide can a sliding door be?

Significantly wider than hinged — commonly up to around 1200mm per door. Fewer doors, cleaner look, lower fitting cost.

Which finish is most popular?

Standard mirror, by a mile — it's the best value-for-impact upgrade. Black-frame frosted glass is the fastest riser for modern renovations, and painted Shaker-profile leads in Hamptons-style homes.
