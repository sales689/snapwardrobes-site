    Common Questions

    Straight answers, before you ask.

    How much does a custom wardrobe cost?

    Built-in wardrobes start from around $1,150 and walk-ins from around $3,500, in white melamine. Size, drawers, doors and finishes move the price — which is exactly why the first step is an in-home design consultation with a fixed, itemised quote.

    Should I choose sliding doors or hinged doors?

    Two things decide it: space and budget. Sliding doors preserve floor space in tighter bedrooms and cost less to fit; hinged doors give you full visibility into the wardrobe but need clearance to open and are generally more expensive. We'll walk you through both at your design consultation.

    What goes in first — the wardrobe or new flooring?

    Carpet: wardrobe first, so the carpet can be replaced later without touching the wardrobe. Timber or floating floors: flooring first, so there are no visible expansion joints or beading around the wardrobe. We'll confirm the right order for your renovation.

    Do you remove my old wardrobe?

    Yes. Removal and disposal can be included in your quote. Keep in mind that removing an old wardrobe can expose patches of unpainted wall — we can add backing and side panels to cover this, or leave time for you to patch and paint before install.

    What if there's a powerpoint in the way?

    An active powerpoint inside the wardrobe cavity needs to be relocated or disconnected by an electrician before installation. If you need one, we can recommend trusted local sparkies.

    Can I get a coloured or premium-finish wardrobe?

    Absolutely. We work with the full Polytec range — from standard melamine through to woodmatt finishes. As a guide, coloured finishes add roughly 25–40% over standard white, depending on the board you choose.

    How strong is the shelving?

    We use quality board and proven shelving systems designed for real household loads — and every drawer runs on soft-close runners as standard. If you have something unusually heavy in mind, tell your designer and we'll build for it.

    Can you quote from plans for a new build?

    Yes — builders and owner-builders can send plans through for an estimate. For a formal fixed quote we measure on site once the build reaches lock-up/gyprocking stage, so the numbers reflect the finished room.

    Is the measure and design really free?

    Yes. We measure, design the layout with you and give you a fixed quote — free, with no obligation to proceed.

    What warranty do I get?

    Every installation is backed by our written 10-year workmanship warranty. If something's not right, we come back and fix it.
