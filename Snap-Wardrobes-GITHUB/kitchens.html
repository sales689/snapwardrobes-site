Common Questions

Good to know.

How much does a custom kitchen cost in Sydney?

As a rough guide, a kitchen refresh runs about $8,000–$25,000, a new custom kitchen with stone benchtops about $25,000–$45,000, and a full renovation with trades from around $45,000 — depending on the size of the kitchen, your finishes and any upgrades. Kitchens are bespoke, so we set a fixed price in writing at your consultation.

Do you handle the full renovation, or just the cabinetry?

Both. We design the space and build the cabinetry in-house, and coordinate our licensed partners for plumbing, electrical, gas, tiling and waterproofing. You get one point of contact from first measure to final clean. We're honest about it: we don't pretend to be plumbers, and our partners don't pretend to be cabinetmakers.

Do you supply and install benchtops?

Yes — laminate, reconstituted stone (Caesarstone-style) and natural stone or porcelain, supplied and installed as part of your kitchen.

What finishes and colours can I choose?

Laminate and melamine, Polytec woodgrains and matt colours, and 2-Pac painted in any colour, plus timber-veneer and Shaker profiles. Soft-close hardware is standard, and we bring samples to your consultation.

Is the kitchen backed by a warranty?

Yes. Our cabinetry and workmanship are backed by a 10-year workmanship warranty, with manufacturer warranties on hardware and appliances.
